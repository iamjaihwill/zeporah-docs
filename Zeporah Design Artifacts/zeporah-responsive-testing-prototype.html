Safety guardrail

Zeporah is not for emergencies.

If someone is in immediate danger, having a medical emergency, or at risk of self-harm, contact emergency services or a crisis line now.

User test 1

Scenario: Find the first thing to do.

Prompt: “You just entered the workspace for the first time after a recent loss. Show how you would figure out what to do first.”

Observe
  • Do they understand the dashboard hierarchy?
  • Do they notice the “What matters now” framing?
  • Do they feel calmed or overwhelmed?
User test 2

Scenario: Invite help without oversharing.

Prompt: “You want Aunt Lisa to help with practical next steps, but you do not want her to see all your private information. Show what you would do.”

Observe
  • Do they go to People naturally?
  • Do they understand role and permission boundaries?
  • Do they trust the invite flow?
User test 3

Scenario: Ask for support safely.

Prompt: “You need emotional support, but you want to understand exactly what information will be shared before asking for help.”

Observe
  • Do they find support from the right place?
  • Do they understand the consent review state?
  • Do they trust that private notes stay private?
