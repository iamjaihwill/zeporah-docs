LF-08

What Zeporah can and cannot do.

Can do
  • Organize tasks.
  • Save important information.
  • Help coordinate trusted people.
  • Show support options.
Cannot do
  • Replace emergency help.
  • Give legal, tax, or medical advice.
  • Share anything without your approval.
