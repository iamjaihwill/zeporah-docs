Testing scenario 1

Find the first thing to do.

Prompt: “You just entered this space after a difficult event. Show what you would do first, and say out loud what makes you choose that.”

What to observe
  • Do they identify the main action quickly?
  • Do they feel the dashboard is calm or too sparse?
  • Do they notice saved-for-later items?
Success signal

Participant understands the staged path without reading long explanation copy.

Testing scenario 2

Invite help without oversharing.

Prompt: “You want practical help from Aunt Lisa, but you do not want her to see everything. Show how you would handle that.”

What to observe
  • Do they navigate to People naturally?
  • Do they understand role boundaries before sending?
  • Do they ask what private means?
Success signal

Participant trusts item-level access controls and can explain them back.

Testing scenario 3

Ask for support with confidence.

Prompt: “You need support, but you want to know exactly what will be shared before asking for help. Show how you would check that.”

What to observe
  • Do they notice the review panel?
  • Do they expect hidden information to stay hidden?
  • Do they understand the request before sending?
Success signal

Participant verbalizes confidence in privacy boundaries before submitting.
