Private by default

A private place to organize what comes next.

Zeporah helps families move through practical next steps without exposing private information or forcing everything into one overwhelming checklist.

What this helps with
  • Know what matters now.
  • Keep notes and documents in one place.
  • Ask for help without oversharing.
Trust model

Your family space stays private unless you choose to share.

You control access
People only see what you allow.

Roles and visibility can change later.

Support is consent-based
Nothing is sent automatically.

Requests are reviewed before anything leaves your space.
